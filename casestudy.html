Switch Case Study:
Fashion / Beauty Website · Concept Design Showcase

KINGS BIRYANI\'S

A modern and elegant website concept designed for a luxury fashion and beauty brand, prioritizing editorial aesthetics and mobile fluidity.

< 1.0s Target Load Time
98/100 Lighthouse Target
320px–4K Responsive QA
kingsbiryani.in

OVERVIEW & SCOPE

Kings Biryani's and Family Restaurant website was designed to give the restaurant a premium digital presence with a cinematic homepage, digital menu, and WhatsApp ordering for customers in Tuni, Andhra Pradesh. The architectural focus is displaying high-resolution runway imagery without compromising mobile bandwidth.

🏢
Project Concept & Domain: Kings Biryani's and Family Restaurant & Beauty — Concept design prototype for luxury retail & cosmetics.

PROJECT GOALS

01 Craft a refined, editorial-grade visual lookbook experience.
02 Ensure seamless, lag-free mobile browsing across varying network speeds.
03 Highlight curated seasonal collections with minimalist product cards.
04 Achieve sub-second page transitions and rapid visual rendering.

DESIGN & DEV

🎨 Design Strategy

Guided by warm ivory backgrounds, charcoal typography, and subtle terracotta accents, Kings Biryani's website balances rich food photography with clear navigation and a premium restaurant aesthetic.

⚡ Front-End Architecture

Engineered with semantic HTML5, modern CSS custom properties, and modular vanilla JavaScript. Zero framework bloat guarantees instant load times and fluid mobile touch interactions.

KEY CAPABILITIES

✓ Interactive seasonal lookbook showcase
✓ Responsive product catalog with instant category filtering
✓ Seamless cart and checkout inquiry flow
✓ Mobile touch-optimized bottom navigation bar
✓ High-DPI responsive image optimization with lazy loading
✓ Subtle micro-interactions on button and card states

TECH STACK

HTML5 Semantic Markup CSS3 Grid / Custom Properties Vanilla JavaScript Responsive Breakpoint QA

MULTI-DEVICE QA

Tested from 320px smartphone displays up to 4K ultra-wide monitors, ensuring flawless typography readability and image proportions at all breakpoints.

💻 Desktop Displays 1024px – 1440px+
📱 Tablet Viewports 768px – 1024px
📲 Mobile Phones 320px – 430px
THE OUTCOME

Quality & Performance Standards

A clean, conversion-ready fashion concept demonstrating sub-second loading speeds, lightweight asset bundling, and 98/100 Lighthouse benchmark performance.
