WHAT I DO

HOW I HELP YOUR BUSINESS GROW ONLINE

You don't need to know code to know what your business needs. Here is exactly what I deliver to make your website an effective business asset.

Commercial Credibility

When clients search for your business, they find a polished, authoritative website that verifies your legitimacy, showcases your past work, and earns their trust immediately.

Frictionless Mobile Browsing

More than 70% of your visitors browse on smartphones. I ensure your menus, service descriptions, and contact options are effortless to read and tap on any phone screen.

Connect visitors straight into your WhatsApp or email inbox with prefilled inquiry messages, eliminating confusing multi-step forms and middleman platform commissions.

TECHNICAL CAPABILITIES

GENUINE SKILLS & TECHNOLOGIES

Practical, reliable web technologies applied to every build. Handcrafted without bloated frameworks for long-term maintainability.

Front-End Architecture

Semantic HTML5 markup, modern CSS custom properties, Flexbox, and CSS Grid. Handcrafted with zero bloated framework dependencies for longevity, security, and effortless hosting.

Multi-Device Responsiveness

Systematic validation across real viewport dimensions from 320px smartphones to 4K desktop screens. Fluid typography (clamp), thumb-friendly touch targets, and zero horizontal overflow.

Performance & Accessibility

Modern WebP image compression, WCAG AA contrast standards, keyboard accessible navigation, structured Schema.org markup, and fast Core Web Vitals scores.
